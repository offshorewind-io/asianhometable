Recipe preview

Bright starters

Choose a course above to preview the dishes. We can adapt the menu for your guests and occasion.

Starters

Lumpiang Shanghai

Crispy Filipino spring rolls filled with shrimp & pork or shrimp & chicken — golden, crunchy, and made for sharing.

Garnaal Kakiage

Lightly battered shrimp and vegetable fritters kissed with Flemish red-ale spices and a whisper of brown sugar for a sweet-savoury edge.

Cod Kinilaw

Fresh cod “ceviche” brightened with citrus and aromatics — a cool, sharp Filipino-style starter.

Main dishes

Chicken Inasal

Char-grilled chicken finished with calamansi and served with crisp atchara pickles.

Chicken Satay

Grilled satay skewers paired with fragrant coconut rice and a fresh side salad.

Hainanese Chicken Rice

Silky poached chicken with aromatic pandan rice — clean, comforting, and full of quiet flavour.

Char Siu Pork

Glazed char siu pork with sesame-soy bok choy and ramen-style egg noodles in a light dashi broth.

Pineapple Humba Adobo

Slow-braised pork cooked in pineapple juice, served with steamed rice and a bright jicama-mango-red cabbage slaw in light honey dressing, finished with kohlrabi, white radish and cilantro.

Beef Caldereta

Rich, slow-cooked beef caldereta with a subtle carbonnade depth — hearty and deeply savoury.

Mechado Tacos

Tender Filipino mechado beef tucked into soft tacos for a playful East-meets-West bite.

Desserts

Banana Turon

Crispy caramelised banana spring rolls served with preferably ube ice cream.

Speculoos Mango Float

Layered mango float with graham crackers, whipped cream, condensed milk and a Speculoos twist.

Mango Sago

Chilled mango sago in coconut and evaporated milk (vanilla ice cream optional).

05 / The people

Behind Asian Home Table

Meet the people
behind the table.

Kim

Kim is a chef from the Philippines who has traveled the world, including Dubai and Australia, and has worked in various restaurants in Belgium. Her cooking brings together those experiences, giving every dish warmth, depth and a personal touch.

Patricia

Patricia is an engineer from Portugal with a thoughtful, practical approach to everything she does. She brings care, precision and a love of bringing people together to the heart of Asian Home Table, helping create welcoming, memorable experiences around the table.
